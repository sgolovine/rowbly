# Comments

A Built-in Plugin that keeps Comments on Stories and Tasks.

## Language

**Comment**:
A note attached to a Story or a Task. Its author is Me; when Me is not set it has no author (`null`).
_Avoid_: reply, remark

**Thread**:
The Comments on one Story or one Task, oldest first; the Comments on a Story do not include those on its Tasks.
_Avoid_: discussion, conversation
