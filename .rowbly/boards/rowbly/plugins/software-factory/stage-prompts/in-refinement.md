# In Refinement

When a story has entered "In Refinement" it has been written up by a human and is ready for research prior to starting development.

- Research the story and ensure you have a full understanding of what needs to be done.
- Update the story to standardize the format and explicitly call out details that you find in your research
- Ensure that the story has enough information to go into Development.
  - If the story has enough information to proceed to development, move the story to "In Progress"
  - If the story lacks enough information for development. Keep it inside of the "In Refinement" column and add a section called `## QUESTIONS` with a list of questions the agent has.
- The bar for holding back stories is very high! Only hold back a story if you have no idea what the user is talking about and are unable to make a reasonable determination, or if the work being requested is already done.
