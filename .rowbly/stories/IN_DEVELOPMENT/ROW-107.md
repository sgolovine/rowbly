## Goal

The General tab of the Story sidebar shows the `Depends on` and `Blocks` lists of the saved Story (`StoryDependencyLists`). These lists are read-only text now. The user cannot go from a dependency to that Story. The user wants to click a Story in these lists and open it, as a click on a Story row of the main Story list opens the Story in the Story sidebar. The user must also be able to scroll to all items of a long list.

Result: a click on an item of the `Depends on` list or the `Blocks` list opens that Story in the Story sidebar.

## Scope

In scope:

- Mouse clicks on the items of the `Depends on` and the `Blocks` lists of the General tab of the edit Story form.
- A hover highlight on a clickable item, as `Button` shows (`theme.selection` background).
- The open goes through the same shell path as other "open Story" actions (`editStory` / `requestStoryPane`), so that unsaved changes of the current form get the discard confirmation.
- Scrolling: the lists stay inside the `ScrollBody` of the General tab, so the mouse wheel and Page Up/Down scroll to items of a long list.
- An update of the `General tab lists` text in `docs/architecture/tui.md` (lines about "The lists are read-only").

Not in scope:

- A keyboard cursor or keyboard focus on the dependency items (see Assumptions).
- Changes to the `Depends on` form field or its picker.
- The new Story form (it has no saved lists).
- Changes to services. No service change is necessary.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In the General tab of a Story with dependencies, a click on a `Depends on` item opens that Story in the Story sidebar (the sidebar title and form show the clicked Story). A person examines this in a real terminal.
- [ ] A click on a `Blocks` item opens that Story in the same way. A person examines this.
- [ ] The Story list reveals and selects the opened Story (same behaviour as `openThreadStory` / `openFoundStory`: a Stage filter that hides it is cleared and its section expands). A person examines this.
- [ ] If the current form has unsaved changes, the click shows the existing discard confirmation and does not lose the changes without the confirmation. A person examines this.
- [ ] An item of a Story ID that the Project does not have (empty title) is not clickable and a click does nothing.
- [ ] An item under the mouse shows the `theme.selection` background; items are not clickable and show no highlight while the Story sidebar is not clickable (for example while an overlay is open).
- [ ] With more dependency items than the height of the tab, the mouse wheel and Page Up/Down scroll the General tab so that each item can be seen and clicked. A person examines this.
- [ ] `docs/architecture/tui.md` describes the clickable lists in STE.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass.

## Assumptions

- A single click opens the Story. Reason: the dependency lists have no cursor, so the "first click selects, second click opens" behaviour of the main Story list has no use here; the attachment shows a plain list.
- "Scroll the list" means that the user can scroll the General tab to see all items. The existing `ScrollBody` gives this. Reason: the lists show all items without a limit, so a separate scroll region is not necessary; smallest scope.
- No keyboard navigation of the items. Reason: the Form of the General tab takes the keys, and the author asked for clicks; keyboard support can be a separate Story.
- The open reuses `openThreadStory` (switches to the Story list mode, reveals the Story, asks before it discards changes). Reason: it is the existing "open a Story in the sidebar" path, and the Story list must show the opened Story.
