Sometimes, the supervisor will not assign a worker to a story in a column where it should have a worker. In cases like this I currently have to take the story out of the stage and put it back in to get the supervisor to "wake up". Fix two things related to this:

- Investegate why this is happening and issue a fix.

- Add a button to the story view. When in an eligible column, there should be a button at the top of the page that a user can press and force the supervisor to evaluate that story for assignment. Should essentially do the same thing as moving the story to the backlog and then back to done.
