- Allow selection of multiple stories.
- Do this via SHIFT + Click or when a user presses the spacebar and then goes up/down with the arrow keys. Pressing spacebar again exits this mode and selects the stories.
- Once stories are selected, you can press SHIFT+ up/down arrow to move them to the next/previous stage.
