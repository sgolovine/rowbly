## Goal

Each Story gets a permanent identity that does not change when its number changes. Today the Story ID (for example `ROW-78`) is the only identity of a Story. When two Story files have the same number, Rowbly cannot know if they are two different Stories or two copies of the same Story. It also cannot know to which of the two Stories a factory attempt, a Thread or an attachment belongs.

Result: each Story file has a `uid` frontmatter key with a UUID. Rowbly writes it when it creates the Story, and never changes it.

## Scope

In scope:

- `createStory` writes `uid` (a random UUID v4) in the frontmatter of each new Story.
- The Story service reads `uid` and gives it in `Story` (`rowbly --json story get` and `story search` show it).
- An update, a Stage move or a re-number keeps the `uid`. Nothing writes a new `uid` for a Story that has one.
- `rowbly doctor` reports each Story without a `uid` as a warning. `rowbly doctor --fix` adds a new `uid` to it.
- `rowbly doctor` reports an invalid `uid` (not a UUID) as an error.
- Update `docs/architecture/storage.md` (the Story frontmatter keys) and `skills/rowbly-reference.md`. Copy the reference to each `skills/<name>/references/rowbly-reference.md`.

Not in scope:

- The use of `uid` in other records (see the Story "Record the Story uid in factory attempts, Threads, outbox entries and attachments").
- The repair of duplicate Story IDs.
- A `uid` in the TUI. The user does not see it.
- A CLI flag that sets or changes a `uid`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A service test: `createStory` writes a `uid` that is a valid UUID, and two new Stories get different values.
- [ ] A service test: an update of the title, the description, the Stage or a custom field keeps the `uid`.
- [ ] A service test: a Story file without `uid` loads, and `Story.uid` is `undefined`.
- [ ] A doctor test: a Story without `uid` gives a warning. With `--fix`, the file gets a `uid` and no other key changes.
- [ ] A doctor test: a Story with an invalid `uid` gives an error and `--fix` does not change it.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass.

## Technical notes

- `src/services/story/story.ts` `newStoryData()` builds the frontmatter of a new Story. Use `ctx` for the random source if the tests need a fixed value.
- `src/services/story-files/story-files.ts` `orderStoryData()` gives the order of the frontmatter keys. Put `uid` after `title`, `stage` and `position`, near `createdAt`.
- The doctor Story checks are in `src/services/doctor/story-checks.ts`.

## Assumptions

- The key name is `uid`, and the value is a lower-case UUID v4. Reason: it is short and it does not look like the Story ID.
- Two Story files with the same `uid` are copies of the same Story. Other Stories use this rule.
