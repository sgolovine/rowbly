Split from ROW-158.

Find and repair the cause when the native factory does not start a worker for a Story in a work Stage. Today the user must move the Story out of the Stage and back to make the factory start a worker. ROW-158 adds a manual "force assignment" control. This Story finds and repairs the cause, so that the manual control is not necessary in normal use.

Findings from the refinement of ROW-158 (start points, not proof):

- The factory loop scans each 30 seconds and on each Story file change (`src/services/factory-manager/factory-loop.ts`). A missed wake-up is therefore not a probable cause.
- The skip reasons that a Stage round trip clears, and that nothing else clears automatically, are in `src/services/factory-policy/factory-policy.ts` and `src/services/factory-manager/manager-status.ts`: `parked` (after `DEFAULT_LIMITS.attempts` = 4 workers that stopped without a Stage move), `retry delay` (up to 60 minutes after failures in a row) and `cancelled by user`.
- `worker stop unconfirmed` stays while an attempt in `.rowbly/state.json` is not in a stopped status. A Stage round trip does not clear it.
- Use `rowbly factory status --json` (`decisions` and `queue`) and the attempts in `.rowbly/state.json` to find the reason for a stuck Story.
