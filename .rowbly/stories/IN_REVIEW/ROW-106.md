## Goal

In the Story list, a user can move only one Story at a time. The user wants to select more than one Story and move all of them to the next or the previous Stage in one step.

Original request of the author:

- Allow selection of multiple stories.
- Do this via SHIFT + Click or when a user presses the spacebar and then goes up/down with the arrow keys. Pressing spacebar again exits this mode and selects the stories.
- Once stories are selected, you can press SHIFT+ up/down arrow to move them to the next/previous stage.

## Scope

In scope (Story list of the TUI, `stories` view mode only):

- A multi-selection of Stories in the Story list. The shell keeps it as a set of Story IDs.
- The range mode: `space` on a Story row starts it. `↑/↓` (and `j/k`) extend the range from the start row (the anchor) to the cursor. `space` again ends the mode and selects the Story rows of the range.
- `shift+click` on a Story row selects the Story rows from the cursor row to the clicked row.
- `shift+↑` moves all selected Stories to the previous Stage. `shift+↓` moves them to the next Stage.
- A visible mark on each selected row, the key hints, the help Modal and the documentation.

Not in scope:

- Other operations on a multi-selection (delete, edit fields, drag of a group).
- Multi-selection in the Factory view (`threads` mode), in the Stage sidebar or in the CLI.
- A new batch service or a new CLI command.
- `ctrl+click` or toggle of single rows.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria are examined by a person in a real terminal (the `rowbly-screenshots` skill), because the project does not test the TUI.

- [ ] In the Story list, `space` on a Story row starts the range mode. The footer shows `space` (`select`) and `esc` (`cancel`) while the mode is on.
- [ ] In the range mode, `↑/↓` and `j/k` move the cursor, and each Story row from the anchor to the cursor shows the selection mark. Stage heading rows in the range are not selected.
- [ ] `space` in the range mode ends the mode. The Story rows of the range stay selected. `esc` in the range mode ends the mode and selects nothing.
- [ ] With no selection, `space` on a Stage heading still expands or collapses the section (no change).
- [ ] `shift+click` on a Story row selects all Story rows from the cursor row to the clicked row, and moves the cursor to the clicked row. It does not open the Story sidebar.
- [ ] A plain click on a row clears the selection, then does the current click action.
- [ ] Each selected Story row shows a mark (for example `●` in `accent`) at the left of its ID. The cursor row keeps its current selection fill and `▌` bar.
- [ ] With one or more selected Stories, `shift+↓` moves each selected Story to the top of the Stage after its own Stage, and `shift+↑` moves each to the bottom of the Stage before its own Stage. The selected Stories keep their relative order in the target Stage.
- [ ] A selected Story that is already in the last Stage (for `shift+↓`) or in the first Stage (for `shift+↑`) does not move. The other selected Stories move.
- [ ] After the move, the Stories stay selected, the target Stage sections are expanded, and a second `shift+↓` moves them one more Stage.
- [ ] With no selection, `shift+↑/↓` and `K/J` keep the current behaviour (move the cursor Story inside its Stage, then into the next Stage).
- [ ] `esc` in the Story list with a selection clears the selection. The Story sidebar does not close. A second `esc` closes the Story sidebar as now.
- [ ] If a move fails, the TUI shows the error as a toast, reloads the board, and the Stories that moved stay moved.
- [ ] The selection clears on a Project switch. After a reload, IDs of Stories that no longer exist go out of the selection.
- [ ] The footer hints of the Story list show `space` (`select`) and, with a selection, `shift+↑/↓` (`move <n> Stories`) and `esc` (`clear selection`). The help Modal (`GLOBAL_KEYS`) lists the new keys.
- [ ] `docs/architecture/tui.md` describes the multi-selection in STE.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass.

## Technical notes

- Keys: `src/tui/shell/shell/shell-keys.ts` (`listKey`, `storyKey`, `headingKey`, `escapeKey`). Today `space` on a heading folds the section, and `shift+↑/↓` (`reorderDelta` in `src/tui/keymap.ts`) calls `shiftStory`. When the selection is not empty, send `shift+↑/↓` to the new multi-move. In the range mode, take `space` and `esc` before the other Story list keys. `enter`, `d` and `n` act on the cursor row only.
- State: add the selection (a set of Story IDs) and the range anchor (or `null`) to the shell state (`src/tui/shell/shell/use-shell-state.ts`, `shell-state.ts`, `shell-view.ts`). Compute the range from `view.rows` (only `kind: "story"` rows).
- Moves: add a function next to `moveStoryStage` in `src/tui/shell/shell/story-changes.ts`. Call `updateStory` from `src/services/story/story.ts` one Story at a time, in sequence (not in parallel), because each call rewrites the positions of the Stage. For `shift+↓`, give positions 1, 2, 3, … in list order for each target Stage. For `shift+↑`, append each Story at the end of the target Stage in list order. Reload one time at the end. Follow `storyPlaced` for the Stage filter and `fold`: when a Stage filter is on, the filter follows the target Stage of the first moved Story.
- Mouse: `src/tui/mouse.ts` drops the modifier bits of SGR reports (mask `0b1100_0011`). Add a `shift` flag (`(button & 4) !== 0`) to `MouseEvent` for a press. Give the flag to the `onClick` handler through `src/tui/hooks/mouse.ts` and `SelectionRow` (`src/tui/components/selection-row/`), for example `onClick(event: { shift: boolean })`. Other users of `onClick` must keep their behaviour. `clickRow` in `src/tui/screens/stories/story-list/story-list-drag.ts` handles the shift case.
- Row mark: `src/tui/screens/stories/story-row/story-row.tsx` gets a `marked` prop, passed through `story-list-item` and `story-list`. Use only `theme` colours (read the `rowbly-tui-design` skill).
- Hints: `src/tui/shell/shell/shell-hints.ts`, `GLOBAL_KEYS` in `src/tui/keymap.ts`.
- Docs: `docs/architecture/tui.md` (section on the Story list keys).
- Do not add tests in `src/tui/` (see `docs/architecture/testing.md`). If a pure helper is placed in `src/services/`, add a test next to it.
- Read `.agents/skills/rowbly-code-style/SKILL.md` and `.agents/skills/rowbly-tui-design/SKILL.md` before the change.

## Assumptions

- `shift+↑/↓` moves the selected Stories only when the selection is not empty. With no selection, the current single-Story reorder stays. Reason: the keys already have a meaning, and the author asked for the new meaning only "once stories are selected".
- `shift+↑` goes to the previous Stage and `shift+↓` to the next Stage, with the same target positions as `ctrl+shift+↑/↓` (top of the next Stage, bottom of the previous Stage). Reason: this agrees with `moveStoryStage`.
- The selection can include Stories of more than one Stage. Each Story moves one Stage from its own Stage. Reason: the range follows the visible rows of All Stages, and this is the smallest rule.
- The Stories stay selected after a move. Reason: the user can then move them again through more Stages.
- `shift+click` selects the range from the cursor row to the clicked row, as in usual list UIs. A plain click clears the selection. Reason: the author did not give the rule, and this is the common behaviour.
- Many terminals (for example kitty, xterm, GNOME Terminal) keep `shift+click` for their own text selection, so the app can never get the event there. The keyboard range mode is the path that always works. Reason: this is a limit of the terminal, not of Rowbly.
- `esc` clears the selection before it closes the Story sidebar. Reason: a user must have a key to clear the selection, and `esc` already cancels.
- The multi-move calls `updateStory` once for each Story from the shell, and adds no batch service. A failure in the middle leaves the moved Stories moved. Reason: smallest scope, and it follows the current `story-changes.ts` pattern.
- The feature applies only in the `stories` view mode. Reason: the Story keys already do nothing in the Factory view.
- After the merge of `origin/main` (ROW-95), Stories in a Stage are in `updatedAt` order and have no settable position. The multi-move now puts each Story at the top of its target Stage for both directions, and writes the Stories in reverse list order to keep their relative order. Without a selection, `shift+↑/↓` changes the Stage of the cursor Story, as on `main`. Reason: the positions of the acceptance criteria no longer exist on `main`.
