## Goal

The Stage heading row of the Story list and the section heading row of the Factory view have almost the same JSX: `SelectionRow` > `Box paddingRight={1}` > the collapse glyph (`▾`/`▸`), the bold label, and `(count)` with an empty note. Two copies can drift apart. Merge them into one shared `SectionHeadingRow` component in `src/tui/components/`, so that the two screens show the same heading from one source.

Current files:

- `src/tui/screens/threads/thread-section-row/thread-section-row.tsx` (`ThreadSectionRow`, used in `src/tui/screens/threads/factory-view/factory-view.tsx`)
- `src/tui/screens/stories/stage-heading-row/stage-heading-row.tsx` (`StageHeadingRow`, used in `src/tui/screens/stories/story-list-item/story-list-item.tsx`)

Differences between the two copies today:

- The empty text: `No Threads` or `No Stories`.
- The optional `spaced` top margin (Thread sections only). The margin is outside the highlight of the selection.
- The count colour: the Thread row uses `theme.inkMuted` when selected and `theme.inkFaint` otherwise. The Stage row always uses `theme.inkFaint`.
- The Stage row passes `RowDragProps` to `SelectionRow`, so that a Story can be dropped on the heading.

## Scope

In scope:

- A new component `SectionHeadingRow` in `src/tui/components/section-heading-row/` (`section-heading-row.tsx` and `index.ts`), exported from `src/tui/components/index.ts`.
- Props: `label`, `count`, `collapsed`, `selected`, `clickable`, `onClick`, `emptyText`, `spaced?` (default `false`) and the optional `RowDragProps` (`drag`, `onDrop`, `onDragOver`, `onDragLeave`), passed through to `SelectionRow`.
- Replace `ThreadSectionRow` in `factory-view.tsx` and `StageHeadingRow` in `story-list-item.tsx` with `SectionHeadingRow`.
- Delete the folders `src/tui/screens/threads/thread-section-row/` and `src/tui/screens/stories/stage-heading-row/`.

Not in scope:

- A shared `sectionedRows<T>()` builder for `src/tui/screens/threads/factory-view-rows.ts` and `src/tui/screens/stories/story-list-rows.ts`.
- A shared `handleSectionKey()` for the fold keys in `src/tui/screens/threads/factory-view/factory-view-actions.ts` (`sectionKey`) and `src/tui/shell/shell/shell-keys.ts` (`headingKey`).
- Any change to the row data, the keys, the mouse behaviour or the layout of the two screens.

## Assumptions

- The count uses `theme.inkMuted` when the row is selected and `theme.inkFaint` otherwise, in both screens. Reason: the author's prop list has no prop for the count colour, so one behaviour must apply to both. The muted colour keeps the count readable on the `selection` fill. The only visible change is the count colour of a selected Stage heading.
- The shared `sectionedRows<T>()` and `handleSectionKey()` are not in scope. Reason: the title of the Story is only the merge of the two heading rows, and the author wrote "also consider" for the other parts. The row builders also differ (the Story list has a mode without sections and a section for unknown Stages). A later Story can do that work.
- The component name is `SectionHeadingRow` and its folder is `src/tui/components/section-heading-row/`. Reason: the author gave the name and the folder, and the repository uses one folder for each component.
