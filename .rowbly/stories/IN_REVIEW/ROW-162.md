## Goal

The left sidebar column shows the Stage sidebar ("Stages") at the top and the tag sidebar ("Tags") below it. A drag of the right edge of the Stage sidebar changes the width of the column. A drag of the right edge of the tag sidebar does nothing. The user expects the same result from the two edges.

Result: a drag of the right edge of the tag sidebar changes the width of the two sidebars, the same as a drag of the right edge of the Stage sidebar.

See the screenshot attachment (`image-20261118-0046.png`, two identical copies). It marks the right edge of the Stage sidebar as "Dragging works from here" and the right edge of the tag sidebar as "Does not work from here".

## Scope

In scope:

- The right edge of the tag sidebar resizes the width of the sidebar column beside the Story list.
- An update of the "The size" item in `docs/architecture/tui.md` (section "The tag sidebar") and of the comments that say that only the Stage sidebar changes the width.

Not in scope:

- The width does not drag in the stacked (narrow terminal) layout. Keep this rule for the two sidebars.
- No change to the drag of the top edge of the tag sidebar (the height).
- No change to the drag of the Story sidebar.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `tagSidebarProps` in `packages/cli/src/tui/shell/shell/shell-props.ts` returns `onResize: handlers.resizeStages` when the layout is not stacked, and `onResize: undefined` when `frame.stacked` is true. A unit test can examine this.
- [ ] In the TUI, with a Project that has tags and a terminal width of 100 columns or more, a drag of the right edge of the tag sidebar changes the width of the Stage sidebar and of the tag sidebar together. A person examines this criterion (or a check with the `rowbly-screenshots` skill).
- [ ] A drag of the right edge of the Stage sidebar continues to work.
- [ ] A drag of the top edge of the tag sidebar continues to change its height. A drag at the top-right corner of the tag sidebar does not break the two drags (it can do one of the two).
- [ ] `docs/architecture/tui.md` and the comments in `shell.tsx` and `shell-props.ts` say that a drag of the right edge of either sidebar changes the width of the two sidebars.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass.

## Technical notes

- Cause: `stageSidebarProps` sets `onResize: frame.stacked ? undefined : handlers.resizeStages`. `tagSidebarProps` sets no `onResize`, so `Sidebar` (`src/tui/components/sidebar/sidebar.tsx`) does not render the right `ResizeEdge` for the tag sidebar.
- Fix: in `tagSidebarProps`, add `onResize: frame.stacked ? undefined : handlers.resizeStages`. The two sidebars already have the same `width` (`frame.shownWidth`) and `maxWidth` (`frame.room`).
- `ResizeEdge` (`src/tui/components/resize-edge/resize-edge.tsx`) measures the x of its target box. The two sidebars are in one column, so the x is the same and the computed width is correct.
- The right edge (`right: -1`, `width: 2`, full height) and the top edge (`top: -1`, `height: 2`, full width) of the tag sidebar overlap at the top-right corner cells. `useMouse` (`src/tui/hooks/mouse.ts`) selects one target for a press. Examine that the corner gives one valid drag.
- Comments to update: the comment in `src/tui/shell/shell/shell.tsx` ("Only the Stage sidebar drags its width.") and the JSDoc of `tagSidebarProps` ("only the Stage sidebar changes the width").
- Docs: `docs/architecture/tui.md`, section "The tag sidebar", item "The size". Write in ASD-STE100.
- Read the `rowbly-tui-design` and `rowbly-code-style` skills before the change.

## Assumptions

- The tag sidebar uses the same handler (`resizeStages`) and the same width state as the Stage sidebar. Reason: the two sidebars share one column and one width, and the author wants the drag to work "from both sides".
- A small unit test for `tagSidebarProps` (a pure function) is permitted in `shell-props.test.ts`, the same as `shell-frame.test.ts`. Reason: the acceptance criteria must be examinable by a test, and the rule "no tests for the TUI" applies to components. If the developer decides not to add it, a person examines the criterion.
