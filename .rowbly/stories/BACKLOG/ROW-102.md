## Goal

The TUI shows the worker hosts and the remote Threads, with their connection.

Read the Spec `remote-worker-hosts`, section "Status and TUI". Read the `rowbly-tui-design` skill.

## Scope

In scope:

- The Factory view shows each host: the connection state and the used and maximum slots.
- The `▶` marker of a Story shows the host of a remote worker, and a "connection lost" state.
- The Threads tab has a host column. A remote Thread with a lost connection shows the time since the last contact.
- A key on an abandoned Thread to apply or discard its held entries. A discard asks for a confirmation.

Not in scope:

- Settings. Another Story adds the Hosts section.

## Acceptance criteria

- The TUI reads `status.json` only. It never opens SSH.
- Use only the components of `src/tui/components/` and the colours of `theme`.
- Check the screens in a real terminal with the `rowbly-screenshots` skill.
