The thread status line is written two times.

Files:
- `src/tui/screens/stories/thread-row/thread-row.tsx` (lines 72-79, outcome line 98-104)
- `src/tui/screens/stories/thread-detail/thread-detail.tsx` (lines 78-85, outcome line 87-93)

Both render the glyph from `THREAD_GLYPHS`, the status (in `danger` when it failed), the "waiting for network" note and the `abandonedText` / `lostText` notes. Only the middle part is different: `runsNote` in the detail, `threadName` in the row. The row also makes the status bold when it is selected. The outcome line (`outcome.error ? danger : ...`) is also copied.

Make `ThreadStatusText({ thread, now, held?, connection?, bold?, extra })` and a shared outcome line, so the two copies cannot drift apart.
