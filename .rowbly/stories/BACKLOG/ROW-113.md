## Goal

The records that belong to a Story keep the Story `uid`, so that Rowbly can find the correct Story when its number changes or when two Story files have the same number. Today these records keep only the Story ID:

- the factory attempts in `.rowbly/state.json` (`story`)
- the Threads in `.rowbly/runtime/threads/` (`storyId`)
- the outbox entries of a remote worker host (`storyId`)
- the attachment manifest in `.rowbly/attachments/<ID>/manifest.json`

Result: each new record of these kinds also has `storyUid`. A read finds the Story by `storyUid` when it has one, and by the Story ID when it does not.

## Scope

In scope:

- A new attempt, a new Thread, a new outbox entry and a new attachment entry get `storyUid` from the Story.
- The reads that find the Story of an attempt, a Thread or an outbox entry use `storyUid` first. If no Story has this `uid`, or the record has no `storyUid`, they use the Story ID as today.
- Old records without `storyUid` stay valid. Rowbly does not rewrite them.
- Update `docs/architecture/native-factory/` and `docs/architecture/storage.md` for the new key.

Not in scope:

- `dependsOn` lists of Stories and the Story links of Specs. People write them by Story ID, and they stay that way.
- A re-number of Stories.
- A migration that adds `storyUid` to old records.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A service test for each record kind: a new record has the `storyUid` of its Story.
- [ ] A service test: a record with a `storyUid` finds its Story after the Story file gets a new number.
- [ ] A service test: a record without `storyUid` finds its Story by the Story ID.
- [ ] A service test: a Story without `uid` gives records without `storyUid`, and no error occurs.
- [ ] The board, help, doctor, status and history paths do not load Pi.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass.

## Technical notes

- Attempts: `src/services/factory-state/state-file.ts` validates an attempt (`story`, `stage`, `workerId`). `src/services/factory-manager/` and `src/services/factory-recovery/` read them.
- Threads: `src/services/thread/thread-types.ts` (`storyId`), with `thread-list.ts`, `thread-groups.ts` and `thread-projection.ts`.
- Outbox: `src/services/outbox/outbox.ts` and `outbox-file.ts` write the entries. `src/services/outbox-apply/` applies them on the main machine. A remote worker host can run an older build, so the apply must accept entries without `storyUid`.
- Attachments: `src/services/attachment/attachment.ts` and `src/services/attachment-files/attachment-files.ts`.
- Obey the lock order: never take the Project write lock while you hold the state lock.

## Assumptions

- The key name is `storyUid` in each record. Reason: the records already use `storyId`, and the two keys are then easy to compare.
- `dependsOn` and Spec links keep only the Story ID. Reason: people write and read them in the Story and Spec files, and a UUID there makes the files hard to read. A re-number leaves them with the Story that keeps the ID.
